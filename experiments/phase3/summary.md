# Phase 3 results: baselines

Generated by `experiments/phase3_report.py` from 300 runs (0 method errors). Same data as Phase 2 for D1 (symbolic IV and plain PySR taken from Phase 2); n = 2000; 50 seeds per cell except DeepIV and its distillations (first 20 seeds). Errors are RMS(f̂ − f₀) in units of the in-sample sd of f₀; extrapolation is on [q₉₉, 3 q₉₉] of X. "Beats symbolic IV" is the share of seeds where the method's in-sample error is lower than symbolic IV's on the same data. Curves (sieve, KIV, DeepIV) give no formula, so form recovery does not apply. Runtimes are with two runs in parallel on 8 cores, so they are about twice Phase 2's single-process times.
